Play offline chess against 40 bot characters from around the world and 5 chess legends, from a 250-rated beginner to a 2800-rated master. Undo, hints, game review and a rating that grows as you win. Free, no ads.

Description:
Chess vs Bots is a complete offline chess game: pick an opponent, pick your colour and play. No internet, no account.

40 BOT CHARACTERS + TOP PLAYERS
Meet bots with their own names, flags, drawn portraits, short introductions and playing styles — from a gentle 250-rated beginner who blunders pieces to a 2800-rated master. Then take on five drawn chess legends: Morphy, Lasker, Tal, Capablanca and Alekhine, each with a short biography.

MY PROFILE
Pick your name and country, and choose a picture, take a photo or upload your own.

STARS
Win to earn up to three stars per bot: three with no hints and no undo.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Choose White, Black or Random
• Undo a move, ask for a hint, offer a draw or resign
• Your game in progress is saved automatically

LEARN FROM EVERY GAME
After the game, review your biggest mistakes and the best move you missed, with an accuracy score.

YOUR RATING
Your player rating starts at 800 and grows with your wins.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Many board themes and piece sets, coordinates, flip board
• Dark and light mode, sound and vibration
• 8 languages: English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Nederlands, Български

FREE, NO ADS
The app is completely free: no ads, no purchases, no tracking. Looking for puzzles? A button takes you to our other game, Chess Tactics Gym.

OPEN SOURCE
Powered by Stockfish, running entirely on your device. Chess vs Bots is free software under the GPL v3; the source code is public (link in the About screen).
