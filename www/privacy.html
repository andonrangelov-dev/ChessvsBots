Privacy Policy — Chess vs Bots

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

Chess vs Bots works fully offline. There is no account, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address or location. Your rating, games and settings stay on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your rating, game history, the game in progress, statistics, settings and chosen language are saved locally inside the app. You can erase them at any time by using “Reset statistics”, or by deleting the app. If you add a profile name, country or picture, they are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

No ads, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not use your advertising ID. It has one button that opens our other game, Chess Tactics Gym (chess puzzles), in the App Store or your browser; that page is then governed by its own policy.

Children

This is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

Your rights

We do not keep personal data about you on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete.

Chess engine

The app includes the Stockfish chess engine (GPL v3), which runs entirely on your device. No data is sent to any engine or analysis server.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Developer: Andon Rangelov. Questions about privacy: andonrangelov@gmail.com
