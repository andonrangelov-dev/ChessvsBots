Chess vs Bots — Support

Chess vs Bots is an offline chess game against 40 bot characters from different countries, from about 250 to about 2800 Elo, plus five chess legends. Choose your colour, take back moves, ask for a hint, offer a draw or resign, save a game in progress and review your game afterwards to see your biggest mistakes and best move. Your player rating grows with your wins.

No ads, no purchases

The app is free, has no advertising, no in-app purchases and no tracking. It has one button that opens our puzzle game Chess Tactics Gym.

Technical support

Please write to andonrangelov@gmail.com and include your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description of the problem. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Privacy

The game works offline and has no account. See the Privacy Policy.
